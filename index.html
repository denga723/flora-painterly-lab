Tree: FP_Tree_H_001. Rock: FP_Rock_0_003. Grass: FP_Grass_001 / 005. Flora Paint PRO 1.3, rendered in Blender 5.2.2 EEVEE. The terrain is a gently uneven slab.

Classic and Anisotropic are native Kuwahara modes. Gouache, Watercolor and Colored pencil are custom combinations. The lighting studies use 96 frames with a fixed camera and animated lights.

Stills: 1024 × 1024. Individual videos: 640 × 640, 16 fps. Filter sizes stay fixed in pixels, so strokes appear relatively larger in video. Kuwahara operates per frame; fine details may shift as the light changes.
